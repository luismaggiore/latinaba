We have extensive experience in all phases of Clinical Research

Our clinical operations portfolio spans Phase I (First-In-Man), Phase II, Phase III, Phase IV, Observational (Obs) studies, and Medical Device research conducted to strict international ICH-GCP standards.

  • Phase III: Pivotal international trials with broad patient reach
  • Phase IV: Post-marketing surveillance and real-world evidence
  • Observational (Obs): Registry studies and epidemiologic data
  • Phase II: Early proof-of-concept and safety evaluations
  • FIM / Device: First-In-Man and Class III Medical Device trials
