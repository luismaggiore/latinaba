Who we are

A full-service Contract Research Organization with senior clinical operations teams across Latin America

For more than 17 years, we have partnered with pharmaceutical, biotech, and medical device companies as experts in the region, conducting all phases of clinical trials, Medical Device studies, Observational and Real-World Evidence (RWE) research, and full Clinical Trial Oversight.

We know the regulatory landscape, site networks, and cultural nuances of each country in the region

Every sponsor works directly with senior experts with boutique attention to detail from teams who've run these trials themselves, not just managed them. Shorter lines of communication, faster decisions, and a team that treats your study as a priority.
