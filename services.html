Services at a glance

Seven ways we support your trial, end to end

Clinical Operations & Project Management

Full end-to-end project management, site monitoring, clinical operations execution, and senior oversight embedded in every country across Latin America. Shorter lines of communication, faster decisions, and direct access to senior leadership.

Regulatory Affairs

In-country regulatory experts managing Ethics Committee submissions, Ministry of Health approvals, site identification, and dedicated customs brokers with local know-how for import/export and site distribution logistics.

Clinical Trial Oversight

Independent GCP audits, on-site investigator training, and site-level SOP development for clinical sites, Phase I units, and bioequivalence units backed by a Quality Management System with 60+ validated SOPs.

Medical Experts Consulting Services

Senior medical liaison services, continuous 24/7 medical monitoring, strategic regional Key Opinion Leader (KOL) mapping, and high-precision medical writing for regulatory dossiers.

Medical & Scientific Services

Systematic literature searches, protocol writing for Phase I–IV and pharmacokinetics studies, Investigator's Brochure (IB) development, and comprehensive market research and trend analysis.

Medical Devices Research & Real-World Evidence

Comprehensive trial execution for Class I, II, and III medical devices, observational registry studies, and real-world evidence (RWE) data collection proven by FDA PMA inspections with No Action Indicated (NAI) outcomes.

Quality Programs & CRO/Sponsor Oversight

Resourcing services, independent CRO and sponsor oversight, Rent-a-Monitor programs, safety monitoring & SAE review, co-monitoring, on-the-job CRA training, audit & inspection prep, and strategic clinical trial consulting.
