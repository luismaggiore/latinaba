01 / Overview

The Clinical Regulatory Landscape in Latin America

Latin America has matured into one of the most reliable regions globally for multinational Phase I–IV clinical trials. Both Argentina and Brazil operate robust, highly respected regulatory bodies — ANMAT (Administración Nacional de Medicamentos, Alimentos y Tecnología Médica) and ANVISA (Agência Nacional de Vigilância Sanitária) — aligned with international ICH-GCP guidelines.

Understanding the nuances of each agency’s submission workflow, documentation standards, and local Ethics Committee (EC/IRB) interactions is critical for sponsors aiming to achieve fast First Patient In (FPI).

02 / Argentina

ANMAT Approval Dynamics & Concurrent Ethics Review

In Argentina, clinical protocol submissions to ANMAT run concurrently with Institutional Ethics Committee (Comités de Ética en Investigación - CEI) submissions at lead trial sites. ANMAT requires comprehensive dossier packages including protocol, ICF, Investigator's Brochure, IB amendments, and IMP manufacturing data.

ANMAT Dossier Requirements Checklist

  • Clinical Protocol & Informed Consent Form (ICF) in Spanish
  • Investigator's Brochure (IB) and safety updates
  • Certificate of Analysis (CoA) & IMP manufacturing specifications
  • Local insurance policy documentation under Argentine regulations
03 / Brazil

ANVISA & CEP/CONEP Pathways in Brazil

Brazil represents Latin America's largest clinical research market. Clinical trial clearance involves a dual-layer review process: ANVISA for technical and drug safety authorization (DDCM) and the CEP/CONEP system for ethical clearance.

Recent regulatory streamlining in Brazil has significantly shortened evaluation cycles for Phase III trials and rare disease protocols. Working with experienced local monitors ensures seamless coordination between regulatory authorities, central labs, and investigator sites.

04 / Framework Comparison

ANMAT vs ANVISA Regulatory Snapshot

ARGENTINA (ANMAT)

Concurrent Ethics Pathway

ANMAT and Ethics Committee (CEI) reviews occur simultaneously. Standard approval window averages 60-90 days for clean submissions.

Key Strength

Fastest site activation timelines in LATAM.

BRAZIL (ANVISA)

Dual DDCM Authorization

DDCM technical approval by ANVISA alongside CEP local ethics clearance. Accelerated pathways for rare disease & Phase III trials.

Key Strength

Largest patient recruitment capacity in South America.
