Why LATAM

Are patients available for clinical trials in Latin America?

Yes. The region offers broad patient diversity and access to treatment-naïve populations, which helps sponsors reach enrollment targets faster than in more saturated markets.

Why LATAM

Is the quality of clinical research in Latin America proven?

Yes. Latin America follows ICH-GCP guidelines, with inspection systems active in every country. LatinaBA's own track record includes a routine FDA PMA inspection in Chile for a Class III device that closed with a No Action Indicated (NAI) result, and a routine FDA inspection of an osteoporosis study involving a biologic that also closed with no findings.

Why LATAM

How experienced are investigators in the region?

Investigators across LatinaBA's network are trained in ICH-GCP guidelines and have supported international sponsors across all trial phases and 34+ therapeutic areas.

Why LATAM

How much can sponsors save by running trials in Latin America?

In Brazil and Argentina specifically, sponsors typically see costs at 60–70% of equivalent US trial costs, without compromising on regulatory rigor.

What languages does LatinaBA operate in?

Spanish and Portuguese, covering every country where LatinaBA has legal presence or regional partners.

Why LATAM

Does Southern Hemisphere seasonality matter for trial timing?

Yes — for seasonal indications (respiratory, flu-like illness), Latin America's Southern Hemisphere calendar is offset from the US and Europe, which can accelerate enrollment for seasonally-dependent studies.

Why LATAM

How does Latin America's patient population compare to the US and Europe?

Latin America is home to more than 650 million people, over 80% of them in urban areas — one of the most urbanized regions on the planet, which speeds up patient access compared to more dispersed markets. Industry data points to a stronger patient-physician bond in the region, contributing to dropout rates roughly a third of those seen in the US and the EU. (Source: IQVIA Institute; Statista.)

Why LATAM

What are the main challenges of running clinical trials in Latin America, and how does LatinaBA address them?

Regulatory timelines and customs are the two most common friction points. LatinaBA mitigates regulatory risk with in-country regulatory experts who get submissions right the first time, and manages customs through dedicated brokers with local know-how for import/export and site distribution.

Sponsors get the same point of contact for the life of the study, with a direct line to senior management — not a rotating account team. LatinaBA staff work locally but deliver to international standards, backed by a Quality Management System with 60+ SOPs and validated by more than 50 capability audits.
