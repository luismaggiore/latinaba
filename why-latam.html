01 / Patient access

A region built to enroll

Latin America is home to 650M+ people, over 80% of them urban, one of the most urbanized patient pools on the planet. That density means faster enrollment and lower dropout than North America, with an added edge for seasonal studies: the Southern Hemisphere calendar runs opposite the US and Europe.

02 / Regulatory track record

A track record you can audit

Every country in the region operates under ICH-GCP, with active inspection systems. LatinaBA's own record includes an FDA PMA inspection in Chile that closed with No Action Indicated, and an FDA inspection of a biologic study that closed with no findings. Investigators are ICH-GCP trained across all trial phases and 34+ therapeutic areas.

03 / Cost & proximity

Lower cost, same time zone

Sponsors in Brazil and Argentina typically pay 60–70% of equivalent US costs, cheaper per site than Western Europe, Oceania, or Asia-Pacific. Unlike those regions, Latin America shares the same time zones as US sponsors, so the savings don't cost real-time collaboration.

04 / Where friction shows up

Where it gets hard and how we clear it

Regulatory timelines and customs are where trials most often lose time in Latin America. LatinaBA runs in-country regulatory experts who get submissions right the first time, and dedicated customs brokers who handle import/export country by country, so friction gets absorbed before it reaches you.

05 / Why LatinaBA

One team, for the life of the study

LatinaBA operates in Spanish and Portuguese everywhere it has presence or partners. Sponsors keep the same point of contact from start to finish, with a direct line to senior management, not a rotating account team. Local presence, run on international standards.
